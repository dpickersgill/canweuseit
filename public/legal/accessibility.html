Accessibility statement

Last updated {{UPDATED}}.

{{ENTITY}} wants CanWeUseIt to work for everyone. It aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, the standard most US public bodies are asked to meet.

What we do

  • Every screen is checked with an automated accessibility test (axe-core) at desktop and phone sizes before each release.
  • Everything can be done with a keyboard, and every control has a label a screen reader can read.
  • Text and controls meet AA color contrast, and the layout works at 200% zoom and on small screens.
  • Status messages are announced to screen readers.

Known limits

  • Drawing a field's outline on the satellite map needs a mouse or touch. Fields can be chosen without the map, from the field picker or a list.
  • Satellite images have no text alternative; field names and sections are always also shown as text.

Tell us about a problem

If something is hard to use, email {{CONTACT}}. We reply within 5 business days, and will help you get your booking done another way in the meantime.
