Privacy policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}. {{ENTITY}} ("we") runs CanWeUseIt, a service that governing groups use to share out sports fields with clubs, schools and other organizations.

Who decides what happens to your data

Each governing group that uses CanWeUseIt (for example a league, school district or town) controls the data about its fields, groups and bookings. We process that data on its behalf. If you have a question about a booking, ask the group that runs the fields. For anything about this policy, contact us at {{CONTACT}}.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and your mobile number only if you add it for text alerts.
  • Group details: the names of organizations and teams, who manages them, and insurance or other documents a group uploads.
  • Bookings: requests, approved times, closures, permits, invoices and payment status.
  • Technical data: one sign-in cookie, and the internet address used to create a new group (to stop abuse).

Do not enter information about children in CanWeUseIt. Team names such as "U10 Girls" are fine; players' names, ages or contact details are not needed and should not be entered.

How we use it

Only to run the service: signing you in, sending booking emails and texts you asked for, showing schedules, issuing permits, and taking payments. We do not sell data, show ads, or use it to train AI models.

How long we keep it

While the governing group uses CanWeUseIt. When a group closes its account, we delete its data within 90 days, except records we must keep by law (such as payment records). Backups roll off within 35 days.

Your choices

You can change your name and phone number, and turn texts off, under Your settings. Reply STOP to any text to stop them. To see, correct or delete your data, email {{CONTACT}}; we answer within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each governing group's data is kept separate by the database itself. Sign-in uses single-use email links instead of passwords.
