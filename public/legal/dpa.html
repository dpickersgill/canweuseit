Roles

The governing group is the controller of its data. {{ENTITY}} is the processor and acts only on the group's documented instructions, which are these terms and the group's use of the service.

What we promise

  1. Process the data only to provide CanWeUseIt, and never sell it or use it for advertising or AI training.
  2. Keep it confidential and limit staff access to those who need it to support the service.
  3. Protect it: encryption in transit and at rest, database-level separation between groups, single-use sign-in links, daily backups, and audit logs of staff decisions.
  4. Use only the sub-processors listed in the privacy policy, and give 30 days' notice before adding one.
  5. Tell the group without undue delay, and within 72 hours, after we learn of a breach affecting its data.
  6. Help the group answer requests from people to see, correct or delete their data.
  7. On request or when the group leaves, export its data as CSV files and delete it within 90 days, backups within a further 35 days.
  8. Keep data in the United States.

Student data

CanWeUseIt does not need student records. Schools should enter only staff contacts and team names. If a school needs a signed student data privacy agreement, contact us.
